title: Working with alerts
description: Triage, acknowledge, and resolve alert events.
---

## View open alerts

1. Open **Alerts** in the sidebar.
2. Filter by state (open, acknowledged, resolved).
3. Select an alert to see monitor context and the triggering run.

## Acknowledge

**Acknowledge** when someone is actively investigating. This signals to the team that the incident is owned without implying the underlying issue is fixed.

## Resolve

**Resolve** when the monitor is healthy again or the incident is closed. Resolved alerts remain in history for auditing.

## Snooze / suppress

During planned maintenance, use snooze or suppression options (when available for your monitor) to reduce alert noise. Document maintenance windows according to your team’s process.

## Copilot assistance

Rythm may offer AI-assisted summaries for failures and suggested alert messages from run context. Use these as a starting point — always verify against run detail.

Ensure [notification settings](/docs/guides/notification-settings) route alerts to the right channels.
