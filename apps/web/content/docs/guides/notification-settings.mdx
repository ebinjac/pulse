---
title: Notification settings
description: Configure Slack and email alert delivery from the console.
---

Alert notifications are configured in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Slack

1. Obtain an **incoming webhook URL** from your Slack workspace (not the channel link alone).
2. Paste the webhook URL in Rythm notification settings.
3. Use **Test** to send a sample message and confirm delivery.

<Callout type="info">
  A Slack channel link opens the channel in the app, but Rythm needs a webhook URL to post messages.
</Callout>

## Email

Configure recipient addresses for email alert delivery. Test notifications verify SMTP or provider integration configured for your Rythm environment.

## What triggers notifications

Notifications fire when monitors breach **alert policy** and create alert events. Tuning monitor alert policy reduces noise — see [Creating a monitor](/docs/guides/creating-a-monitor).

## Alert message content

Rythm can generate alert messages from run context. Review messages in the **Alerts** detail view; copilot-assisted drafting may be available for your environment.
